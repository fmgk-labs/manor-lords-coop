# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**.
Below is a short version in plain words. Where it differs from the full text, the full text counts:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, social and economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We act in ways that make an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Good behaviour

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give helpful feedback, and take feedback well.
- Own your mistakes, say sorry to the people affected, and learn from them.
- Think of what is best for the whole community, not only for you.

## Not acceptable

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Posting other people's private information (for example a home address, an e-mail address, a Windows user name or a Steam ID) without their clear permission.
- Anything else that would be clearly wrong in a professional setting.

## Who enforces it

The maintainers ([docs/MAINTAINERS.md](docs/MAINTAINERS.md)) make clear what is acceptable and act when something is not.
They may remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues and other posts that break this code, and they say why when it helps.

## Where it applies

In every space of this project: this repository, the Discord server, the comments on the Nexus Mods page,
and when someone speaks for the project in public.

## How to report

Write to **`<CONTACT>`**. Every report is read and looked into quickly and fairly.
The maintainers keep the name of the person who reports private.

## What can happen

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Unkind words or other behaviour that is out of place | A private, written warning that says what was wrong. A public apology may be asked for. |
| 2. Warning | One serious case, or several smaller ones | A warning, and no contact with the people involved for a set time. Breaking it can lead to a ban. |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious case, or behaviour that goes on | No contact with the community for a set time. Breaking it can lead to a permanent ban. |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of breaking the rules, harassment, or attacks on groups of people | No contact with the community, ever. |
