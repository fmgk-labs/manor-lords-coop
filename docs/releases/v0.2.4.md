Manor Lords Co-op 0.2.4 for Manor Lords 0.8.117 (Steam).

**Every player needs this same version.** A copy on another version is refused at the join. Install it the same way as before: unzip into `Manor Lords\ManorLords\Binaries\Win64` and overwrite.

## New in 0.2.4

- Building maintenance now follows the host: a friend's buildings no longer drop to "Needs Maintenance" while the host's are maintained. The maintenance slider is shared both ways.
- Bandit theft happens only on the host: the "resources stolen" banner and the lost goods are the same on every copy, or neither appears.
- Notices come from the host: a friend no longer gets notices its own game made up, and the host's notices show the same words and building on every copy.
- Spoils of war: one offer on each copy after a bandit camp is looted, and one answer closes it everywhere. If two players answer in the same second, the first answer counts and the other player is told.
- Includes everything of 0.2.3.

## How it was tested

- Two game copies on one PC: joins, single reloads, resyncs, a full rejoin, about 150 game days at speed 4 and 8 with no resync asked and no crash.
- The clean zip, installed as a player does, with real mouse clicks: the maintenance panel and slider on both copies, notices, the bug report button, the Main Menu guard.
- Not tested yet over real Steam between two PCs. Please report what you see.

## Known issues

See [docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md](https://github.com/fmgk-labs/manor-lords-coop/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md). New in this list: a notice can show twice on a friend for a few seconds (G8), the game's "Cold!" window can open on a friend only (G9), the building panel's "Yield" line can differ (G10).
