Manor Lords Co-op 0.2.7 for Manor Lords 0.8.117 (Steam).

**Every player needs this same version.** A copy on another version is refused at the join. Install it the same way as before: unzip into `Manor Lords\ManorLords\Binaries\Win64` and overwrite.

## New in 0.2.7

- **Visit mode.** Press **B** to walk on the map as your lord. Every other player sees you walking, with your name above you. Press **B** again, **Enter** or **Escape** to go back. Hold **Left Shift** to run. The game's own **Explore** button starts it too, and the game speed stays as it was. Change the key in the game: **Settings > Controls > "Walk (co-op visit mode)"** (a second key can be set there too). To switch visit mode off on your copy, open `coop.cfg` in the mod's folder and remove the `#` in front of `visit = off`.
- Stock: every building's goods on a friend, in your town and in rival towns, follow the host's within about 1 to 2 seconds. Region totals arrive faster.
- Hand carts and timber carts on a friend follow the host's: who carries or pulls one, and which stand holds one. A friend's villager or ox no longer picks up a cart by itself (G18).
- A friend no longer keeps extra free merchants after a reload (G16). A rival lord's livestock merchant now walks to his town on a friend too, with the animal beside him.
- An ox that dies on the host dies on a friend too, and its body shows there (G20).
- A friend's claim is checked by the host first: no claim while enemies are in the region (G21).
- Two players changing one region's trade limit, trade rule or production limit in the same second: both copies end on the same value (G22).
- A friend's Explore button, and the game's second alarm a few seconds after an enemy is spotted on a friend, no longer drop the game to speed 1 for everybody.
- Two players demolishing one building at the same time: no reload and no double refund (this was in 0.2.6 too).
- A friend's building, field or pasture on ground the host has just built on is refused, and the friend sees the host's instead within about half a second (no reload). A friend's move of a building the host is demolishing at that moment is refused the same way.
- Upgrades, backyard extensions and artisan workshops: a friend shows them after the host, never first, and never one the host's game did not carry out.
- A building that changes its type when upgraded (a storehouse, a house that grows) keeps its stock right on a friend.
- Castle wall joins are carried, and the castle planner window redraws after another player's change.
- The rival towns' construction bars follow the host's.
- Trees felled, logs, stone, berries, fish and mushrooms on the ground follow the host's.
- Mercenary offers: a friend's own monthly roll no longer changes the list; the host's list is shown.
- The production log, the "goods made" numbers and the time pie follow the host's.
- Fertility: a patch of fields could stay different on a friend for good (this was in 0.2.6 too). Fixed.
- A raid warning shows the host's start day. Quests whose name is not their kind's usual one now reach a friend.
- Join and leave notices show on every player's copy.
- A soldier's body after a fight lies in the same region as on the host. A new building reaches a friend's lists in its first moment.
- A rival lord's short-lived squads, and the leftover of a failed bandit camp, leave a friend within about a second (G32). A rival house's upgrade list matches the host's (G31).
- The foreign market's supply and prices reach a friend right after each new day.
- A friend replaying a rival's new house waits for the game to make it, so the site gets the host's work from the start.
- Two safeguards around felled trees cleared under a new building site (F7).
- A rare close of a player's game in a busy town (buildings and piles coming and going) is fixed (this was in 0.2.6 too).
- Includes everything of 0.2.6.

## How it was tested

- Two game copies on one PC: session checks of about 18 minutes at speeds 4 and 8 with no resync and no crash; goods and stock numbers equal within the link delay at every reading. Final check of this exact build: goods 100 % in all three phases (a host's change shown on the friend within 1.2 s at most), 0 resyncs, no crash; every panel of both test saves (68 and 58 panels, about 45,000 values) equal on host and friend.
- Three copies on one PC (host and two friends): town actions, stock and settings equal on all three.
- Visit mode with real keys and clicks on test copies: walking, running, leaving, the Controls row and its key change.
- Not tested yet over real Steam between two PCs, visit mode included. Please report what you see.
