Manor Lords Co-op 0.2.6 for Manor Lords 0.8.117 (Steam).

**Every player needs this same version.** A copy on another version is refused at the join. Install it the same way as before: unzip into `Manor Lords\ManorLords\Binaries\Win64` and overwrite.

## New in 0.2.6

- Notices: a friend's notice list now shows the host's notices (some were missing).
- An open Trade tab, storage filter tab or Limits tab shows another player's change at once (the number stayed old until the panel was opened again, and a + or - then started from the old number).
- Opening a field's panel no longer sends a "fertilize" change by itself.
- A demolition can no longer make a friend resync (the building's name could be lost on the way).
- Another lord's army on a friend: squads that left are gone within about a second (it took 4), and a new army appears whole.
- A rival town's new livestock shows on a friend within a second.
- Animals: when a sheep, cow or other farm animal dies on the host, it dies on a friend within about a second and its body shows there (it took 30 seconds, with no body).
- New villagers appear on a friend within a moment, also after a friend reloads.
- A mine collapse: the ruin shows on a friend within half a second.
- Includes everything of 0.2.5.

## How it was tested

- Two game copies on one PC: about 150 game days at speeds 4 and 8 with no resync and no crash; goods numbers equal within the link delay at every reading; the panel comparison on two saves (about 25,000 values a copy).
- The clean zip installed as a player does, and a pass with real mouse clicks.
- Not tested yet over real Steam between two PCs. Please report what you see.
