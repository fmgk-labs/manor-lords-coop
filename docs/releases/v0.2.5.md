Manor Lords Co-op 0.2.5 for Manor Lords 0.8.117 (Steam).

**Every player needs this same version.** A copy on another version is refused at the join. Install it the same way as before: unzip into `Manor Lords\ManorLords\Binaries\Win64` and overwrite.

## New in 0.2.5

- Open panels redraw when another player changes something in them: building panels and tabs, region and lord panels, the development tree, claims, the army and mercenary panels. Not yet: a trade limit in an open Trade tab (G19).
- A friend's trade log on the Trading Post is the host's full history (it showed only part of it).
- The production log, the building panel's "Yield" line, field fertility, region graphs, prices and expertise follow the host.
- Notices: the doubled notice on a friend is gone; "Cold!" and "Hunger!" come from the host; the full notice list after a reload.
- Deaths, fires and new homes follow the host in any order; a dead villager's body leaves a friend's town on the host's day, also after a friend reloads; burials show on a friend about a second after the host; a mine collapse collapses on a friend (it could burn down before).
- Fields, the construction bar, building collapse, maintenance and family rolls follow the host.
- Soldiers: no stop-and-go march on a friend; a player's own order keeps his men; line drags land where the host's do; villagers taken late into an army no longer risk a crash on a friend.
- Free merchants and their pack animals stay after a friend reloads.
- Villagers on a friend get their task within a moment after the host sends people home.
- The host's messages are read faster on a friend (less work per frame).
- Includes everything of 0.2.4.

## How it was tested

- Two game copies on one PC: joins, reloads, about 150 game days at speeds 4 and 8, the panel comparison on two saves (about 25,000 values a copy), goods numbers equal within the link delay.
- Not tested yet over real Steam between two PCs. Please report what you see.
